01Research

Research Projects

Since March 2025 I have worked as a Research Support Officer on two projects at the University of Malta, researching current AI methods, writing deliverables and publishing the results.

Project ACCLAIMED

AI Content Cloning for Language-Agnostic Media for Education Democratisation

A content generation tool that lets educators create slides, videos and assessments for educational settings.

  • Generative AI
  • Audio-visual cloning
  • Education

Project WAVE

Water rescue and Artificial Intelligence within a Virtual reality Environment

A VR simulation of Maltese beaches developed in Unreal Engine and paired with Generative and Explainable AI, providing realistic water rescue scenarios that can be used for training.

  • Virtual reality
  • Unreal Engine
  • Large language models
